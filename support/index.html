The three games

  • Make 24 — combine four numbers with + − × ÷ to make exactly 24.
  • Krypto — use four cards with + − × ÷ to hit the target shown on the fifth.
  • Nerdle — six guesses to find the hidden 8-cell equation; green = right digit & spot, yellow = right digit wrong spot.

Frequently asked

How do I solve a puzzle?

In Make 24 and Krypto, tap a number tile and drag a line to another tile — a ring of + − × ÷ buttons appears; tap one to combine them. Repeat until one tile equals the target.

What is the Daily Puzzle?

One puzzle per game per day, the same for every player worldwide. Solving it builds your streak. Tap the calendar icon to replay the last 30 days from the Past Dailies archive.

What are streak freezes?

Miss a day and a snowflake "freeze" token is spent automatically to protect your streak. You start with three. Tap the snowflake badge on the Daily screen to see how many you have left.

Why are there ads?

Quick Maths is free, and banner ads keep it that way. Heads-Up two-player mode is ad-free for everyone. There are no in-app purchases.

How do I reset my progress?

All progress lives on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears every score, streak and stat. There is no cloud backup, so a reinstall starts fresh.

Why does the app ask for tracking permission?

It's Apple's standard prompt. Allowing it lets ads be personalised; declining it shows non-personalised ads instead. The game plays identically either way. Change it anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Does Quick Maths work offline?

Yes — every game, including the Daily Puzzle, works fully offline. Only ads need a connection.
